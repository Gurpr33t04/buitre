ADMISSION NOTICE 2026

Admission to the Master of Technology programmes for the session 2026 will be conducted through the centralised counselling process announced by the university.

Candidates who have appeared in the qualifying examination and are awaiting results may also apply, subject to submitting proof of passing before 31/07/2026.

The last date for submission of the online application form along with the prescribed fee is 30/06/2026 and no extension will be granted under any circumstances.

Hostel accommodation is limited and will be allotted strictly on merit; applicants must submit the separate hostel form together with the admission application.
