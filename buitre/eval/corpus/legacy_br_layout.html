Links		 The Department of Physics was established in nineteen sixty-two and has since grown into one of the leading centres for condensed matter research in the region.

Our faculty supervise doctoral students across optics, solid state physics and computational methods, with several joint projects funded by national agencies.

The department library holds over eleven thousand volumes and subscribes to forty journals, and is open to all registered students from nine until five.
