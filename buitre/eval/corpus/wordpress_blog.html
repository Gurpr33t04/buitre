How we bake sourdough

Posted on February 11, 2026 by Maria Costa

Our sourdough starter is fed twice a day with equal parts flour and water, and we keep it at a steady twenty-five degrees all year round.

After mixing, the dough rests for thirty minutes before a series of four gentle folds spread across the next two hours of bulk fermentation.

Shaping is the step most home bakers rush, but a tight surface tension is what gives the finished loaf its tall, open and slightly glossy crumb.

We bake in preheated cast iron pots at two hundred and forty degrees, lids on for twenty minutes and lids off for another twenty-five minutes.

Let the bread cool completely before slicing, because the crumb is still setting and cutting early will leave it gummy and dense in the middle.
